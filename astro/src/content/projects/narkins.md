**Narkin’s Builders **is a modern real estate website and lead generation platform developed to showcase projects, publish updates, and convert visitors into qualified sales inquiries. We built a fast, SEO focused Next.js platform with a premium user experience and a structured content workflow that allows the team to manage listings and pages independently without relying on developers.

The platform includes a headless CMS powered by TinaCMS, CRM style lead capture workflows, PWA capabilities for an app like experience, and SEO infrastructure designed to support long term discoverability and growth while maintaining a clean, high trust brand presence online.
