Loom is a full stack legal collaboration platform built for law firms and in house legal teams. We developed a real time collaborative drafting system, enterprise authentication infrastructure, and a Pakistan focused AI legal research engine within a single production grade platform.

Built with Next.js and Go, the system supports secure workflows through JWT authentication, Google OAuth, and WebAuthn passkeys. Loom AI uses a Retrieval Augmented Generation architecture powered by Chroma to retrieve Pakistani legal statutes and user uploaded documents before generating source grounded responses.
