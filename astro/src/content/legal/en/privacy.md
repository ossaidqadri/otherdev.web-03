## Who we are

Other Dev ("we", "us") is a digital studio based in Karachi, Pakistan. This policy covers the website at otherdev.com. For anything about your data, email hello@otherdev.com.

## What we collect

We don't collect anything you type on this site: it has no sign-up, contact or comment forms, and no accounts.

We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It records page views, referrers, country and performance timings. It sets no cookies and does not build a profile of you or follow you across sites.

Our host, Cloudflare, also sees your IP address and browser details in its normal server logs, the same as for any website it serves.

## Getting in touch

Links on this site open WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or your email app. What you send there is handled by those services under their own policies, and by us when we read and reply to it. We keep project conversations for as long as the work or relationship needs.

## Sharing

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the service providers named above, and where the law requires it.

## Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@otherdev.com. We reply within 30 days.

## Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top.
